Design exactly THREE distinct weathered sandstone stone pillars of different heights, connected by two irregular stone lintels forming a coherent ancient ruin with two bays. Chunky dimensional masonry, chipped corners, carved runes, restrained moss, cracks, muted ochre/olive stones. Main left bay is a LARGE OPENING FILLED COMPLETELY by an opaque aubergine/violet/plum magical curtain with soft lavender swirling veins and layered depth; absolutely no see-through forest or empty transparent hole in main bay. Secondary narrower right bay smaller, also purple energy. Gentle restrained glow at inner stone edges, no neon bloom. Top-down three-quarter game perspective, looking down ~35 degrees. CRUCIAL: the MAIN aperture plane recedes diagonally from rear upper-left to foreground lower-right, so creatures leave towards screen RIGHT, never towards screen bottom. Rear pillar ground around canvas (160,620), middle foreground pillar foot around (620,920), third right-rear pillar foot around (870,680). Main exit ground anchor near (370,730), with a clear rightward walk lane through this spot. Main aperture tall and wide enough for a humanoid golem; clean readable silhouette at 130-160 game pixels. Frame occupies most square canvas with 5% transparent padding, all stone top and feet visible. No ground tile, no floor platform, no scenery, no grass island, no character, no text labels, no UI, no black oval shadow. Contact shading painted only directly on the feet of stones. True transparent background around the portal including between exterior stones. Original cohesive hand-drawn artwork, not photorealistic, not pixel art, not glossy 3D.

Runtime overlays animate faint vector ribbons over the existing opaque purple artwork. The front pillar uses the same PNG and an SVG clip path; no raster repainting, alpha deletion or derived cutout image is performed.
